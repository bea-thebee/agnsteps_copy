# Bin-Width Monte Carlo Comparison

In [[ t.ipynb]], cell 9 runs 100 Bayesian-block simulations for select bin widths. This can be randomized, but to test the values of 24, 27, 30, 33, and 36 days were used. It summarizes and plots the average block count, step time, variability TS, and step ratio for each width. This was created to compare the average variability and block sizes to that of just sampling the 30 day bins

Cell 10 compares the sweep averages with a 30-day-bin baseline, displaying the summary metrics and cell counts in tables and side-by-side plots. Related context: [[Monte_Carlo_monthly.ipynb]] and [[discovery.ipynb]].
